About Us

Who Are We?

நல்லோர் வட்டம் (Nallor Vattam) is a grassroots community organisation in Tiruppur, Tamil Nadu, fighting for a waste-free city.

We are ordinary citizens — fed up with overflowing garbage, illegal dump yards, and zero accountability. We organise mass cleanups, community protests, and awareness campaigns to force change in Tiruppur.

Street & public space cleanups
Protests against illegal dumping
Demand accountability
Waste segregation awareness
Why Join Us

Be Part of Something Real

Not a social media page — actual boots on the ground, cleaning Tiruppur ward by ward.

Real Community

Thousands of ordinary Tiruppur citizens working together — students, workers, homemakers — all united for one clean city.

Every Ward Covered

We operate in 24 zones across Tiruppur. From Selvapuram to Rayapuram — no area is too far for our volunteers.

We Raise Our Voice

Beyond cleaning — we petition, protest, and demand accountability from corporations and the municipality.

Official Membership

Receive your Nallor Vattam member certificate, get first access to events, and build a lasting network of change-makers.

Our Mission

What We Fight For

Two clear goals. One united community. Zero tolerance for waste.

01

Waste Clearance

We physically remove waste from streets, parks, water bodies and public spaces across every ward in Tiruppur.

  • Street & roadside cleaning
  • Drain & canal desilting
  • Vacant plot cleanups
  • Post-event waste collection
02

Protest & Demand Change

We raise our voice against those who dump waste illegally. We demand proper infrastructure and strict enforcement.

  • Peaceful street protests
  • Petitions to authorities
  • Expose illegal dump yards
  • Social media campaigns
